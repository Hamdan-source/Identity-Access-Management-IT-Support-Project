## Situation
A new employee, Jonas Eriksson, was given a company laptop 
on his first day. IT was required to enrol the device into 
Microsoft Intune, ensure it met company security compliance 
and push a software update policy before he could begin work.

## Action
I opened the ticket and confirmed the requirements with the 
team. I then set up the Apple MDM Push Certificate in Intune 
to allow macOS device management.

I created a compliance policy requiring password protection 
and device encryption, then enrolled the MacBook into Intune 
via Company Portal. The device was verified as compliant.

I then created and assigned a software update policy to 
ensure the device stays current with security patches.

## Reasoning
Intune MDM ensures devices meet security standards before 
accessing company resources. The compliance policy enforces 
zero-trust principles — the device must prove it is secure 
before being trusted on the network.

## Result
Device successfully enrolled and verified compliant. 
Compliance and update policies applied. Jonas Eriksson 
laptop confirmed ready for use on his first day. 
Ticket resolved.
