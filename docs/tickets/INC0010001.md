# INC0010001 - HR User Account Disabled

## Ticket Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Ticket Number | INC0010001 |
| Date | 2026-05-01 |
| Caller | Carol Johnson |
| Department | HR |
| Category | Access Issue |
| Priority | 1 - Critical |
| State | Resolved |

## Situation

Carol Johnson from HR couldn't log into her workstation at 8:00 AM during payroll processing. Since HR is time-sensitive during this period, access issues had an immediate business impact.

## Action

I opened the ticket in ServiceNow and assigned it to myself right away. I called Carol to confirm the issue and let her know I was already working on it.

I checked her account in Active Directory and found it was disabled. I re-enabled the account, reset her password, and set it to require a change at next login.

Once done, I verified access and asked Carol to confirm she could log in before closing the ticket.

## Reasoning

I prioritised the ticket immediately because HR access issues during payroll are business critical.

Disabling and re-enabling accounts is a standard control, but I still verified everything before making changes to avoid unnecessary disruption.

I kept communication open so the user was not left waiting without updates.

## Result

Carol regained access and was able to continue payroll work without delay. The issue was resolved within SLA and the ticket was closed after user confirmation.
